Features

Everything you want.
Nothing in your way.

The power of Chromium with the tools you actually use built right in.

AI in your sidebar

Chat with your favorite assistant next to any page, signed in with your own account. Select text and ask about it in one click.

ChatGPTClaudeGeminiGrok+ any site you add

Tabs your way

Vertical tabs in the sidebar or a classic strip on top. Pin, drag, sleep and group them into workspaces.

Ads and trackers, blocked

A fast built-in blocker with a one-click switch per site. Plus HTTPS-only mode and Do Not Track.

Chrome extensions

Install from the Chrome Web Store with “Add to Kite”. Your favorites just work.

Move in, in seconds

Bring your bookmarks, history and logins along from Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge and more.

Fast by keyboard

A command palette for everything, rebindable shortcuts, mouse gestures and smart address-bar autocomplete.

Customize

Make it unmistakably yours.

Kite is built to be changed. Every color, corner and shortcut is yours to pick. Try a theme on the window above.

  • Themes and accents. Six built-in themes, a theme editor, and import/export.
  • Layout editing. Reorder the toolbar, move the sidebar, go compact or auto-hide.
  • New tab backgrounds. A scenic photo every day, gradients, colors or your own pictures.
  • Custom CSS. Restyle the browser itself, or any website you visit.

Performance

You decide how much it gets.

Set a RAM and CPU budget with two sliders. Kite sleeps and freezes background tabs to stay inside it, so the tab you’re using stays fast.
